Announcement & Origin

Official Notice

This section is for the short announcement from USPolopatrons.org that explains what the USPPA is, how Polo Patronium fits in, and why the token exists as a stewardship tool.

Polo Patronium (PATRON) is issued as a living token of support and tradition within the United States Polo Patrons Association. It is designed to coordinate tribute, rewards, and recognition across chapters, horses, hosts, and patrons in the Cowboy Polo ecosystem.

Patronium Model

Stewardship Framework

This section summarizes the overall model you described: stewardship-based rewards, tribute allocation, and claim-based distribution rather than staking or yield framing.

Polo Patronium operates as a stewardship-based coordination tool rather than a speculative product. Rewards, when granted, are discretionary acknowledgments of contribution across the Cowboy Polo ecosystem—not guaranteed returns.

Claim, Don’t Stake

No Farms, No Lockups

This is where you explain why you are not using staking contracts, but rather snapshot-based, claim-driven distributions.

  • No lockups, no staking contracts, no “yield farms.”

  • When rewards are granted, they are claimed directly, not streamed from a staking pool.
