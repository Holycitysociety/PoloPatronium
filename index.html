INITIATIVE ROADMAP

USPPA
STRING 7̶7̶7̶ REMUDA

A shared remuda of US PPA horses — either consigned to the Association or owned by it — entering the USPPA herd, assigned to operating patrons, and developed for play and training across our programmes.

COWBOY POLO CIRCUIT

A national endeavour to broaden the sport’s reach, nurture emerging talent, and encourage the next generation of American players — where riders not only learn to play, but to train and help train the horses of the String 7̶7̶7̶ Remuda.

THE POLO LIFE

A platform dedicated to presenting the elegance and traditions of polo to new audiences in the digital age — following our horses, patrons, and players across the Remuda, Charleston Polo, and the Cowboy Polo Circuit.

CHARLESTON POLO CLUB

The renewal of Charleston, South Carolina’s polo tradition — our flagship Chapter and living test model for the USPPA Polo Incubator, where horses are gathered, pasture secured, instruction established, and the public welcomed to learn and play. Once an Incubator achieves steady operations, sound horsemanship, and visible community benefit, it becomes a standing Chapter of the Association.

All of these initiatives are coordinated and supported through Polo Patronium, the living token of patronage within the United States Polo Patrons Association, uniting patrons, players, and clubs in a shared economy of sport.
